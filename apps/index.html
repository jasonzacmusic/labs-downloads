Chart Studio

Six handwritten gig charts, digitised in their original layout. Transpose for any instrument or to any key, play any section on real piano samples, dictate music by voice, write with an Apple Pencil, and correct anything by right-clicking it. New charts, imports and reordering are all in the sidebar.

Trumpet Transposer

Your handwritten concert-pitch exercise sheets, redrawn and transposed for B♭ trumpet. Same engine as Chart Studio now: every sheet is its own project, add or import more, reorder them, drag sections around, and edit any note on the staff.

Both apps are single self-contained files — they keep working with no network once loaded. Your edits are saved in the browser you open them in, so each machine keeps its own copy.
